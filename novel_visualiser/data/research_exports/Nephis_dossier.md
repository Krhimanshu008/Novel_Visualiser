### Chapter 3144+ [APPEARANCE]
> According to Cassie, Nephis' true essence is a being woven out of pure light and flame. Her human body is not presented as her true self, but rather a form she takes instead of the natural foundation of her existence. Unlike ordinary beings, whose mortal flesh serves as the natural foundation of their existence, and therefore must endure the gradual and painful process of being reforged and reborn as a more perfect being in order to truly become Supreme. Nephis transformed almost instantly because she was unique in her very nature.
